Overview

The Workshop on Low-Cost Ocean Instrumentation convenes experts in ocean instrumentation, sensor development, embedded systems, and related engineering disciplines to accelerate the development of miniaturized, scalable, and affordable observing technologies for the marine environment.

The workshop's emphasis is on discussion, collaboration, demonstrations, community building, and cross-disciplinary interaction, with the goal of exchanging ideas, fostering new collaborations, demonstrating prototype systems, and identifying future research directions.

Dates & Venue

Venue: Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution, Woods Hole, MA, USA. Demonstrations and facility tours will be hosted at WHOI's DeepTech facility.

Main Workshop

October 19–22, 2026

Monday–Thursday. Invited presentations, a keynote lecture, lightning talks, panel discussions, demonstrations, breakout discussions, and collaborative roadmap planning.

Optional Hands-On Development Session

October 23 & 26–28, 2026

Friday, then Monday–Wednesday. An optional, engineering-focused extension where participants get hands-on with components and help build working examples of open-source, low-cost instruments, alongside collaborative prototyping, testing, and demonstrations.
